Night Coast

Every light on the world's coasts, flashing its charted rhythm.

Light data © OpenStreetMap contributors, from the seamark tags mapped by OpenSeaMap volunteers. The light list this page is built on is a derived database under the ODbL: lights.js. Coastlines from Natural Earth. For looking at, not for navigation. Rhythms are drawn from each light's charted character, period and sequence.
