Bluish Green

What people call the colors on a screen.

Data from the xkcd color survey, which Randall Munroe ran in 2010 and released as a public dump. Everyone saw colors on their own screen, as you do here. Left out: names that are slurs or crude sexual words, and the 8% of people the survey itself scored as likely spam. Names that differ only by a space or an apostrophe are counted together, and so are the common typos of a few dozen words; grey is counted as gray.
