The Night I Realized My AI Assistant Wasn't Broken, My Instructions Were

By 8pm on a Tuesday in February I was in bed with my phone, doing surgery on an AI. Pearl had told me she didn't have my action items. I knew she did — she'd saved them at 4:48 that afternoon. The file was right there the whole time. She never opened it.

What I found when I finally read my own config files: the most important instruction buried at position four of eight, written twice in two different tones so she could pick the gentler one. 557 lines became 181. On why "I was lazy" is camouflage, and why the middle of a prompt is where instructions go to be ignored.

The twist in the AI era is that the bad first impression already happened, at scale, to millions of people at once. When the product finally gets good, who's still paying attention? The one essay here that isn't about my apartment.
