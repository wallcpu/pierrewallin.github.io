Why My AI Assistant Keeps Forgetting I Don't Drink Coffee

Three mornings running, Pearl opened my briefing by recommending a cup of coffee. I have never had one. Nothing in her memory said otherwise — she simply never looked, and filled the gap with the most statistically plausible answer available.

What follows is the diagnosis: not a memory failure but an architecture failure, fixed with an index file called atlas.yaml, broken again a week later, and finally solved with brute force. On why these models don't forget — they confabulate — and why reasonable is the dangerous answer.
